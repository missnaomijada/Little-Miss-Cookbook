Homemade Pepperoni Pizza

Golden base, crisp edges, molten mozzarella and pepperoni.

Open recipe

Crust

  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 2 Tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp active dry yeast
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 cups plain/AP flour

Sauce

  • 1 × 6 oz can tomato paste
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp onion powder

  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder

Food Science 🧪

Roux School

Equal-ish flour and fat is the starting pattern. White roux keeps more thickening power; longer-cooked blond/brown roux develops nuttier flavour while gradually losing thickening strength.

Browning

Maillard: amino acids + reducing sugars create savoury roasted complexity. Caramelisation: sugars break down and transform under heat. They overlap in a kitchen, but they are not the same process.

Emulsions

Oil and water normally separate. Emulsifiers and technique can hold tiny droplets together. Heat, ratios and agitation explain why some sauces split.

Flavour Balance

Think sweet, salty, sour, bitter, umami, fat, heat, aroma and texture. “Flat” does not automatically mean “add salt.” Diagnose first.

Luxury & Depth

Browned butter, fond, reductions, stock, slow onions, mushrooms, aged cheese, citrus zest, herbs, spices and deliberate texture contrast create depth.

Why Food Feels Heavy

Perceived richness can rise with concentrated fat, dense starch, sugar, cream sauces and portion size. Acidity, herbs, vegetables, bitterness and crunch can make a rich plate feel more balanced.

Save My Food 😭

Diagnosis → why → safest correction → what not to do → next-time prevention.

🌶️ Too spicy

Rescue

First identify the heat. For chilli/capsaicin, increase the unspiced base or total volume. Dairy/fat can soften perceived chilli heat where it belongs in the dish. Starchy accompaniments can rebalance the meal. Sweetness or acid may balance certain dishes, but neither is a universal eraser.

🧂 Too salty

Add more unsalted base/volume when possible. A potato is not a magic salt vacuum.

🍋 Too sour

Dilute where possible, or balance with richness and sometimes a small amount of sweetness, depending on the dish.

🥣 Too watery

Reduce uncovered if the dish tolerates it, remove excess liquid, or use the appropriate thickening method rather than throwing flour straight in.

🧀 Grainy cheese sauce

Lower the heat. Avoid boiling after cheese goes in. Add suitable cheese gradually. Prevention beats trying to force a broken protein network smooth.

🔥 Burnt bottom

Immediately transfer the unaffected food to a clean pan. Do not scrape the burnt layer into the good food.

🍚 Mushy rice

Stop cooking and vent steam. You cannot truly uncook it. Record liquid ratio, heat and timing for the next attempt.

🍗 Dry meat

Lost moisture cannot be magically restored. Improve serving with sauce or jus; next time control internal temperature and resting.
